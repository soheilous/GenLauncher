A modern mod launcher for
Command & Conquer: Generals and Zero Hour

Install, switch between and start mods, patches, add-ons and alternate executables, then press Play. Your game folder is put back the way it was when the game closes, even after a crash.

Get it on GitHubPrivacyTerms

Mods from their authors

Nothing is mirrored: the launcher opens a mod's own page (ModDB, Nexus Mods, an author's site) and you download it from there.

Safe by design

Changes to your game folder are journaled and undone when the game exits, or at the next start after a crash.

Your settings, anywhere

Export them to a file, or keep versioned backups in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive. Optional.
